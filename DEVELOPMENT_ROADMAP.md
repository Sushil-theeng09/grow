# Development Roadmap

## Status

Proposed sequence for discussion, not a committed delivery schedule. Milestone scope and priority should be approved before implementation.

## Milestones

### 0. Product and technical discovery

- Confirm target MVP learner journey and first supported user segment.
- Define measurable product outcomes, content boundaries, privacy expectations, and accessibility target.
- Evaluate and approve the initial technology stack and deployment approach.
- Convert high-level backlog items into testable, scoped stories.

**Exit:** MVP scope, key decisions, and acceptance criteria are approved and recorded.

### 1. Functional foundation

- Establish application structure, development workflow, configuration, and automated checks.
- Implement identity, access control, profile, and preferences as required by the approved MVP.
- Deliver a basic responsive and accessible learner experience with explicit loading and error states.

**Exit:** A learner can safely access the initial experience, and critical paths have automated tests.

### 2. Goal-based learning

- Implement goals and the first structured content model.
- Connect courses, modules, lessons, and resources to an initial learning pathway.
- Record activity and show meaningful progress.

**Exit:** A learner can follow a pathway and see progress backed by persisted activity.

### 3. Practice and assessment

- Add practice activities, projects, and assessments.
- Capture assessment attempts and evidence, identify gaps, and provide transparent next steps.

**Exit:** Assessment and progress behavior is validated, explainable, and covered by tests.

### 4. AI-assisted guidance

- Select approved AI use cases, provider, context policy, and quality thresholds.
- Add a constrained learning assistant and/or recommendations with evaluation, usage controls, and failure behavior.

**Exit:** AI behavior meets agreed quality and safety criteria and has operational monitoring.

### 5. Ecosystem and operations

- Expand discovery, personalization, analytics, and administrator capabilities based on validated need.
- Mature integrations, scale, monitoring, recovery, and operational processes.

**Exit:** Each expansion is supported by user evidence and operational readiness.

## Delivery gates

Every milestone must have an approved scope, acceptance criteria, risk review, test strategy, and updated documentation. Public deployment, paid services, and release actions require explicit approval.
