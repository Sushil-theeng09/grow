# Grow Project Vision

## Identity

- **Name:** Grow
- **Product:** A complete personal learning and development ecosystem

## Vision

Empower every individual to discover their potential, develop meaningful capabilities, and continuously grow throughout their personal and professional journey.

## Mission

Transform how people learn and develop by bringing together quality educational resources, intelligent AI guidance, practical learning experiences, and measurable personal progress in one unified platform.

## Who Grow serves

Grow is for people across educational and professional domains, including school and university students, independent learners, working professionals, job seekers, career changers, entrepreneurs, educators, and lifelong learners. The product must not be restricted to technology, programming, AI, or data science.

## Product philosophy

Grow should support a connected development journey:

1. Discover interests, opportunities, and learning possibilities.
2. Understand existing knowledge and capabilities.
3. Establish personal learning and development goals.
4. Create personalized learning pathways.
5. Access structured educational resources.
6. Learn with AI-powered guidance.
7. Practice through interactive activities and projects.
8. Evaluate knowledge and capabilities.
9. Identify skill gaps.
10. Improve through adaptive recommendations.
11. Track personal and professional development.

## Product principles

- Personalize for the learner's context, goals, and current capabilities.
- Connect discovery, learning, practice, assessment, and progress rather than treating them as isolated features.
- Support broad subjects and varied career and life goals.
- Make learning progress observable and meaningful.
- Use AI as a transparent aid to learning, not as a substitute for verified content or learner judgment.
- Design for accessibility, responsive use, privacy, and secure handling of learner data.
- Build incrementally while preserving a path toward the complete ecosystem.

## Boundaries and open questions

The vision does not select a technology stack, define a business model, prescribe a content licensing approach, or settle detailed assessment, personalization, and AI policies. These require explicit product and technical decisions before implementation.
